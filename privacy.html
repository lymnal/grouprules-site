GroupRules

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · applies to the GroupRules browser extension and this website.

Short version: GroupRules does not collect, store, sell or share your browsing data. It has no analytics, no cookies, no account and no servers of its own. Tab addresses are matched against your rules on your own computer and never leave it. The only network request the extension ever makes is checking a Pro license key with our payment provider, and only after you choose to activate one.

Who is responsible

GroupRules is made and sold by Ethan Sam, an individual in New York, USA ("I", "me"). Contact: ethanesame+grouprules@gmail.com.

What the extension accesses, and why

  • Web addresses of your open tabs (web browsing activity). Used only to decide which tab group a tab belongs in, according to rules you write. This happens entirely inside your browser. Addresses are not recorded, logged or transmitted. This is why Chrome shows the "Read your browsing history" permission.
  • Your rules and settings (group names, site names and patterns you type, colours, toggles) are saved in your browser's extension storage. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your own devices under Google's privacy policy. I never receive them.
  • Short-lived working data kept in your browser's session memory and erased when the browser closes: the last 20 grouping actions (tab numbers and positions, for Undo) and the site name of any tab you manually moved out of a group (so GroupRules respects your choice).
  • A local counter of how many tabs GroupRules has grouped, and the date your free trial started, kept in your browser. Used only to show the trial status and, once, a review request. Never sent anywhere.

The one network request: license check

If you buy Pro and paste your license key, the extension sends that key and GroupRules' public organization identifier to Polar (api.polar.sh), our payment provider, to confirm the purchase. It re-checks occasionally (when the browser starts or you open the popup). Chrome asks your permission before the first check. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to Polar. No browsing data is ever included.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Polar Software, Inc. as merchant of record and authorized reseller. Polar processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; I never see your card number. Polar shares with me the order details needed to support you (such as your name, email address, country and what you bought). I use them only to deliver support and refunds and to keep the tax and accounting records the law requires. I never sell, rent or use them for advertising.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts or fonts. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations under its own privacy policy. When you uninstall GroupRules, Chrome opens a short "why did you leave?" page here. It records nothing; if you choose to email feedback, I receive your email address and message.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. GroupRules uses browsing activity only to provide its single purpose — putting tabs into tab groups using your rules — does not transfer it to anyone, does not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or any other purpose, and no human reads it.

Children

GroupRules is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your rules any time in GroupRules' settings, or uninstall the extension to remove all of its local data.
  • To access, correct or delete purchase information I hold, email me. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights; I will honour them. I do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change would let GroupRules collect more data, it will ask you in the extension first.
